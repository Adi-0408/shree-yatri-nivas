Terms of Stay

Property Policies & Guidelines

Child Policy (< 4 Free)

Children below 4 years of age stay free of charge when sharing existing bedding with parents. Additional charges apply for children aged 4 years and above or if an extra bed is requested.

Pay at Property

Our online reservation system requires zero advance payment. You simply reserve your room and pay directly at the reception during check-in via UPI, Cash, or Debit/Credit card.

Non-Refundable Policy

Due to high pilgrimage demand, rooms once confirmed are blocked exclusively for you. Bookings are non-refundable. In cases of genuine travel emergencies, please reach out to front desk staff.
